A letter from the founder

The story behind the link

You won’t find my name on this page, and that’s on purpose.

Studio Index isn’t about me. It’s about you, the seller who posts your products and hopes the right person messages you. I’d rather you judge this platform by what it does for your business than by who built it.

Here’s what I kept noticing: Instagram is a brilliant place to be discovered, but a tough place to run a store. The same questions again and again, “How much?” “Is it available?” Orders buried in DMs, and customers who vanish halfway through a chat.

So I built Studio Index. You upload your products, get one unique link and put it in your bio. Your customers browse, add to cart and order, without you typing the same price fifty times.

The part I’m proudest of is the WhatsApp bot. Orders reach you in the app you already live in, and you manage them with a quick reply. That’s the difference between feeling like a seller and feeling like a business.

I’ll be honest: we’re just getting started. I’m building this step by step and listening closely to sellers. If something doesn’t work for you, tell me, because I read every message.
